What Does Jenie Do?

Our platform offers a complete suite of tools to take the complexity out of your taxes.

Simply enter your income streams. Jenie breaks down the exact amounts you owe or are owed according to the current tax brackets, providing completely transparent calculations.

2. Smart Deduction Tracking

Track your business and personal expenses. Jenie automatically highlights which expenses qualify as tax deductions to ensure you save as much money as legally possible.

Working or spending internationally? Utilize our integrated currency exchange calculator to accurately report and balance foreign income and overseas expenses on your return.

Tax rules change regularly. Our dashboard includes a live news feed offering the latest financial updates so you never miss a deadline or a new legislative benefit.
